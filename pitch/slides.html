02 The insight

Every euro goes into one of three baskets.

Fixed

Mandatory & regular

Plan it.

The bills that come every month, whatever you do.

🏠 Rent💡 Utilities🛒 Groceries🚌 Transit🔁 Subscriptions
Irregular

Mandatory, but surprising

Buffer for it.

Necessary costs that don’t arrive on a schedule.

🦷 Dentist🔧 Car repair💊 Pharmacy🧊 New fridge
Leaks

Slips through your fingers

Train it.

Small everyday choices that add up without you noticing.

🍔 Takeaway🚕 Taxis🎟️ Fun🛍️ Impulse buys🏪 Kiosk
You can’t cut rent today. You can plug a leak.
SPankkiBotDeterministic, explainable classification: category + MCC + amount + recurrence
The single most useful money idea we found is splitting spending into three baskets. Fixed you plan, irregular you buffer for, leaks you train. Every screen, every AI answer and every lesson in SPankkiBot uses this one mental model. Repeated in context, it turns into literacy. The classification is deterministic and explainable: category, MCC code, amount and recurrence, each with a "why".

05 AI architecture

Grounded. Generative. Open.

  • 🔓

    Open-weights, tools + vision, OpenAI-compatible. Self-host in the EU and swap models freely.

  • 📐

    Grounded: the LLM picks the analysis and deterministic SQL computes it. No invented figures.

  • 🛡️

    Private by design: only aggregates reach the model. Writes are confirm-first.

  • 🧩

    Generative UI: answers arrive as interactive widgets, with a sources chip on every answer.

  • 📴

    Resilient: provider chain, then an offline engine over the same tools.

SPankkiBotFastAPI · DuckDB · SSE · Nemotron 3.5 Super VL
The LLM never does maths and never sees raw rows. It decides which analysis to run. Deterministic Python and SQL computes the answer, and the model explains it in persona. Tools also return widgets, so the answer arrives as an interactive what-if slider or a goal plan with a create button, not a wall of text. Writes are confirm-first. And because it's open-weights behind an OpenAI-compatible interface, S-Bank can host it in its own EU data centre and swap models later.
